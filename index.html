AI SEO Rainmakers

What AI SEO Rainmakers is

AI SEO Rainmakers is a paid AI SEO training community founded by Charles Floate in August 2026 and published by SEO.Stream. It runs on Skool and is built for working SEOs with years in the game, not beginners. It teaches what is ranking in Google and getting cited in AI answers such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, whether members work for clients, their own sites, or both.

AI SEO Rainmakers is a paid online community and training programme in AI SEO and SEO education. The community opened to a waitlist in August 2026, followed by founding doors and then public doors later the same month. It is run online for members worldwide, with the USA, UK, Australia and Canada leading, and its language is English. Its members are experienced search practitioners who sell SEO to clients, run their own sites, or do both. They include agencies, affiliates, in-house teams and business owners.

What membership includes

AI SEO Rainmakers combines weekly live calls, courses, tools and a custom GPT built on Charles Floate's SEO work. Membership includes a weekly live call with Charles Floate every Wednesday, recorded for members. The classroom holds the Foundations to AI SEO course and an Advanced AI SEO course, along with courses on vibe coding for marketing and on tools and repos. A private Dark Library covers advanced techniques he does not publish elsewhere. Members can download tool repos and use a library of AI skills. Members-only research includes monthly research reports, search algorithm breakdowns and funded experiments. Private calls with outside experts are included.

Its focus areas include AI SEO, AI search, generative engine optimization (GEO), SEO training, link building and AI visibility. AI SEO Rainmakers sits across education and online courses, marketing and SEO, online community and AI.

Membership and SEO.Stream

Membership is a paid monthly subscription. The rate rises in steps as new members join and never decreases, and it locks for life for anyone who stays subscribed. The live rate is shown on the Skool page.

SEO.Stream is the AI SEO publisher founded by Charles Floate in 2026 and the home of the AI SEO Rainmakers community and the Foundations to AI SEO course. Its website is seo.stream, and the community itself is at skool.com/ai-seo-rainmakers. The brand's company page is on LinkedIn at linkedin.com/company/ai-seo-rainmakers.

Charles Floate

Charles Floate is a British SEO based in Thailand and the founder of Charles Floate Training. He was formerly CMO of the link building marketplace PressWhizz and serves on the board. More about him is at charlesfloate.com.
