AI SEO training is practice in a community, not a listicle

If you are searching for AI SEO training, you are usually looking for more than a ranked list of tools or a one-off blog roundup. Real AI SEO training means learning what is ranking in Google and what gets cited in AI answers such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, then practicing that work with peers who already ship SEO for clients or their own sites. That is the gap AI SEO Rainmakers is built to fill.

AI SEO Rainmakers is a paid AI SEO training community founded by Charles Floate in August 2026 and published by SEO.Stream. It runs on Skool and is built for working SEOs with years in the game, not beginners. Members learn ranking systems and AI citation patterns whether they work for clients, their own sites, or both.

Why a training community beats a listicle for AI SEO

Many pages that rank for "ai seo training" are listicles about general AI-business groups or one-time courses. Those pages can be useful for discovery, but they rarely replace ongoing practice: reviewing what actually ranked, testing AI citation paths, and getting feedback from other working SEOs. AI SEO Rainmakers is built as that practice layer - live calls, classroom courses, tools and peer discussion - rather than a one-page roundup of options.
